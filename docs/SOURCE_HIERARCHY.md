# IP-SAKTI Sahayak — Authoritative Source Hierarchy

To eliminate hallucinations and prevent misrepresenting legal weight, IP-SAKTI Sahayak enforces a 4-Tier Source Hierarchy.

---

## Tier 1: Primary Official Indian Authorities
Authoritative government statutes, official gazettes, and statutory rules.
- **India Code / Legislative Department**: The Patents Act 1970 (specifically Section 3(p) Traditional Knowledge exclusion), Biological Diversity Act 2002.
- **IP India (Office of CGPDTM)**: Manual of Patent Office Practice & Procedure (Traditional Knowledge Examination Guidelines).
- **Ministry of AYUSH**: Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940 (Section 3(h)), Drugs and Cosmetics Rules 1945 (Rule 158B licensing requirements for Patent/Proprietary ASU medicines), Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India (API), Ayurvedic Formulary of India (AFI).
- **National Biodiversity Authority (NBA)**: Biological Diversity Rules 2004, Guidelines on Access to Biological Resources and Benefit Sharing (Form I, Form III approval procedures).
- **FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India)**: Food Safety and Standards (Health Supplements, Nutraceuticals, Food for Special Dietary Use) Regulations 2022.

---

## Tier 2: International Official Authorities
Foreign statutory agencies and multilateral treaties.
- **WIPO (World Intellectual Property Organization)**: Intergovernmental Committee on Intellectual Property and Genetic Resources, Traditional Knowledge and Folklore (IGC).
- **US FDA (Food and Drug Administration)**: Dietary Supplement Health and Education Act (DSHEA) 1994, 21 CFR 101.93 (Structure/Function Claims), 21 CFR 190.6 (NDI Notifications).
- **Australia TGA (Therapeutic Goods Administration)**: Therapeutic Goods Act 1989 (Section 26A Listed Medicines), Australian Regulatory Guidelines for Complementary Medicines (ARGCM).
- **European Patent Office (EPO)**: Guidelines for Examination (Plant extracts & Traditional medicine claims).

---

## Tier 3: Scientific Evidence
Peer-reviewed clinical and scientific literature.
- **PubMed / MEDLINE**: Randomized controlled trials (RCTs), systematic reviews, pharmacokinetic synergy studies.
- **CCRAS & Ministry of AYUSH Journals**: Journal of Drug Research in Ayurvedic Sciences (JDRAS).
- **Peer-Reviewed Phytomedicine Journals**: Journal of Ethnopharmacology, Phytomedicine, Elsevier/Springer nature botanical publications.

---

## Tier 4: Secondary Research & Explainers
- Reputable legal commentaries, IP bar association explainers, industry analyses.
- *Strict Rule: Tier 4 sources are NEVER used to establish primary statutory validity.*
